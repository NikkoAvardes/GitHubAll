# Design — Le Mas Provençal

## Vibe
Warm, editorial, rooted. Inspired by Nestora template: oversized serif typography, asymmetric layout, organic photo crops. Feels like a well-worn Provence farmhouse cookbook.

## Colors
- Background: `#F5EFE4` (warm parchment)
- Surface: `#EDE4D3` (aged linen)
- Accent dark: `#2C1A0E` (espresso brown)
- Accent warm: `#9B3E1A` (terracotta)
- Text primary: `#1E110A`
- Text muted: `#7A6050`
- Border: `#D4C4B0`

## Typography
- Display: `Playfair Display` (Google Fonts) — large, italic, editorial
- Body: `Lato` — clean, readable
- Accent/label: uppercase Lato, letter-spacing wide

## Layout
- Asymmetric sections: text left/image right alternating
- Oversized headings that bleed or overlap
- Generous whitespace between sections
- Full-bleed hero with overlay text
- Cards with no rounded corners — straight edges, raw feel

## Motion
- Fade-in + slight upward translateY on scroll for sections
- No bouncy animations — subtle and dignified

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- No gradients on white
- No rounded pill buttons — use sharp rectangular
- No card grids with equal spacing — vary the rhythm
